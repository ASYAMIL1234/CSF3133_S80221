Our Inventory

The Lantern Keeper’s Daughter (RM50.99)
A young girl inherits her grandfather’s lighthouse and discovers letters hidden in its walls. Each letter reveals a secret about the village and the storm that changed it forever. A heartwarming mystery about family, memory, and courage.
Code of Tomorrow (RM50.99)
In 2090, a self-taught programmer uncovers a flaw in the AI that runs her city. As she races to fix it, she must decide who to trust. A fast-paced techno-thriller about power, privacy, and choice.
Rice Fields at Dawn (RM50.99)
This memoir follows a farmer’s son growing up in rural Malaysia during the 1970s. Through simple, vivid stories, it captures village life, hard work, and the quiet strength of family.
The Little Owl Who Couldn’t Sleep (RM50.99)
Ollie the owl is wide awake when everyone else is fast asleep. With help from his forest friends, he learns that being different isn’t so bad. A gentle bedtime story for children aged 3–6.
